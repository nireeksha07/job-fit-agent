# Targeting and scoring rubric

## Targeting
- **Titles (priority):** Product Manager, Associate Product Manager, Product Manager – Technical, Product Owner
- **Titles (bridge/core):** Technical Product Owner, Product Analyst, Senior Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, Lead BA (prefer product-owner duties), AI / Automation BA
- **Experience band:** postings asking for roughly 4–9 years
- **Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) or remote India; flag on-site roles clearly
- **Companies:** a curated list of product/SaaS companies, big tech, global capability centres and fintechs; IT services and staffing firms excluded

## Score
1. Extract every **must-have** from the job description: years, skills, domain, tools, seniority.
2. Mark each one **met** only if the master resume clearly shows it. Partial ≠ met.
3. `score = met ÷ total × 100`
4. Subtract **5–20 points** per red flag:
   - onsite-only or night shift
   - asks for 10+ years
   - coding-heavy (developer role)
   - a required domain not in the resume
5. **≥ 90%** → build a tailored resume. Below → log as skipped with the reason.

## Tailoring rules
- Use only facts and numbers from the master resume.
- Two pages maximum; lead with the 3–4 most relevant projects.
- Mirror job-description keywords only where a matching bullet already exists.
- Keep the real job title; the headline may lead with the target role.
